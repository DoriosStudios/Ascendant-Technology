This version brings some fixes and rebalances to the Aetherium and Titanium equipment sets, enhancing their performance and usability.

## ITEMS
### Armor
- Aetherium Armor Set
    - Base Stats:
        - Increased Enchantability from 22 to 30 for all pieces.
    - Boots:
        - Added 5 Protection Points (2.5 Armor).
    - Chestplate:
        - Added 10 Protection Points (5 Armor).
        - Added Damage Absorption for Magic and Warden's Sonic Boom.
        - Now hides player's location.
    - Helmet:
        - Added 5 Protection Points (2.5 Armor).
    - Leggings:
        - Added 8 Protection Points (4 Armor).

- Titanium Armor Set
    - Base Stats (all pieces):
        - Now they can be enchanted.
    - Boots:
        - Added 3 Protection Points (1.5 Armor).
        - Increased Durability from 195 to 363.
    - Chestplate:
        - Added 8 Protection Points (4 Armor).
        - Increased Durability from 240 to 561.
    - Helmet:
        - Added 4 Protection Points (2 Armor).
        - Increased Durability from 165 to 429.
    - Leggings:
        - Added 7 Protection Points (3.5 Armor).
        - Increased Durability from 225 to 495.

### Tools
- Aetherium Tools
    - Base Stats:
        - Increased Enchantability from 22 to 30 for all tools.
        - Increased Mining Speed from 12 to 16 for all tools.
    - AIOT
        - Fixed enchantable component to allow axe enchantments instead of pickaxe enchantments.

- Titanium Tools
    - Base Stats:
        - Increased Mining Speed from 10 to 12 for all tools.
    - AIOT
        - Descreased Damage from 11 to 10.
        - Fixed enchantable component to allow axe enchantments instead of pickaxe enchantments.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed Aetherium and Titanium tools not providing any protection.
- Fixed inconsistent durability values for Titanium equipment — values now match diamond equipment correctly.
- Fixed missing enchantable component on certain armor pieces (Aetherium/Titanium pieces now correctly accept enchantments).
