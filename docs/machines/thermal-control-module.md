# Thermal Control Module

[![](../pics/thermal_control_module.png)](../pics/thermal_control_module.png)

Stability and heat management system for high-performance machine operations.

## Purpose
The Thermal Control Module reduces heat and instability generated by machines operating at high speeds, particularly when using Hyper Processing Upgrades (H.P.U) or overclock systems. It prevents machine failures and improves reliability in advanced manufacturing setups.

## Operating Modes

### Passive Mode
- Uses structural materials (Titanium/Aetherium plates) for baseline heat dissipation
- No energy consumption
- Lower heat reduction effectiveness
- Always active when installed

### Active Mode
- Consumes energy and Cryofluid for enhanced heat dissipation
- Significantly improved heat reduction
- Protects against instability events
- Automatically activates when connected to power and fluid supply

## How to Use
1. Place the Thermal Control Module adjacent to high-speed machines
2. Connect to energy network for active cooling
3. Supply Cryofluid via tank or capsules
4. Monitor heat levels and Cryofluid consumption
5. Module automatically prioritizes machines with highest heat generation

## Machine Capabilities
- **Energy Capacity**: 32,000,000 DE (32 MDE)
- **Energy Consumption**: 800 DE/tick when active
- **Processing Rate**: 8,000 DE/tick
- **Fluid Tank Capacity**: 16,000 mB (16 buckets) Cryofluid
- **Heat Reduction (Passive)**: 10% reduction
- **Heat Reduction (Active)**: 40% reduction
- **Effective Range**: Adjacent machines (directly touching)
- **Upgrade Slots**: 3 (Efficiency, Range Extension, Auto-Priority)

## Heat Management

### Heat Generation Sources
- Hyper Processing Upgrades (H.P.U): +15% heat per tier
- Overclock systems: +25% heat per level
- High-speed recipe execution: Variable
- Unstable reactions: +50% heat spike

### Heat Effects Without Control
- **25-50% heat**: Reduced efficiency, minor output degradation
- **50-75% heat**: Increased energy consumption, risk of failed recipes
- **75-90% heat**: High risk of corruption, potential item loss
- **90-100% heat**: Machine shutdown, possible damage to adjacent blocks

### Heat Reduction Formula
```
Final Heat = Base Heat × (1 - Reduction Rate) × Cooling Modifier
Passive: Reduction Rate = 0.10 (10%)
Active: Reduction Rate = 0.40 (40%)
Cooling Modifier = 1.0 with Cryofluid, 2.0 without
```

## Cryofluid Consumption
- **Base rate**: 50 mB per operation cycle
- **Per adjacent machine**: +25 mB per machine being cooled
- **Under high heat (>75%)**: ×1.5 consumption multiplier
- **Maximum consumption**: 500 mB per tick

## Upgrade Effects

### Efficiency Upgrade
- Reduces Cryofluid consumption by 20% per tier
- Reduces energy consumption by 15% per tier
- Maximum: 3 tiers

### Range Extension Upgrade
- Extends effective range to diagonal adjacency
- Allows cooling machines within 2-block radius at tier 2
- Maximum: 2 tiers

### Auto-Priority Upgrade
- Automatically prioritizes machines with highest heat
- Balances Cryofluid distribution intelligently
- Reduces total cooling overhead by 10%
- Single tier upgrade

## Integration with Other Systems

### Works with:
- **Hyper Processing Upgrades**: Enables safe H.P.U usage

- **Network Center**: Reports cooling status and effectiveness

### Requires:
- **Energy network**: For active cooling mode
- **Cryofluid supply**: Via Cryo Chamber or capsules
- **Adjacent placement**: Must touch machines to cool
