# Catalyst Weaver — Recipes

Below are the native Catalyst Weaver recipes. Infuser recipes are also imported automatically and converted to catalyst format (see `BP/scripts/config/recipes/infuser.js`).

## Legend
- Catalysts: up to 6 slots. Extra slots must remain empty.
- Byproduct: chance in %; quantities can be a range.
