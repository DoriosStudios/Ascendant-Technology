# v0.6.0

Version 0.6.0 delivers a new fusion workstation, world ore generation, recipe tweaks, and cleaner UIs across the machine lineup. With that, Titanium and Aetherium ores are now naturally integrated into the world, and several machines have received quality-of-life improvements.

## BLOCKS
### Machines
- **Energizer**
    - Energy Capacity increased from 12.2 MDE to 25.6 MDE.

### Ores
- **Titanium (Deepslate)**: Found between Y-32 and Y-64 in the Overworld as Deepslate Titanium Ore.
- **Aetherium**: Found below Y-56 in the Overworld as Deepslate Aetherium Ore; also spawns in the End.

## ITEMS
- Crafting catalog reorganized: groups for machines and capsules (cryo, liquid Aetherium, dark matter).

## RECIPES
- The following blocks have had their recipes modified or added:
  - Absolute Container
  - Cloner
  - Cryo Chamber
  - Energizer
  - Liquifier
  - Residue Processor
- Added more Energizer recipes for Energized Iron Ingot.

## UI/UX
- Updated UIs for Cryo Chamber, Energizer and Network Center.
- Chest screens now route to machine panels; new borderless backgrounds.
- EN/PT text expanded for new blocks and items.

## ASSETS
- New textures for ores (Titanium/Aetherium) and titanium blocks; Energizer on/off states; atlas mapping updated.
