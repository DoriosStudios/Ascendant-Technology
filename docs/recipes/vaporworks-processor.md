# Vaporworks Processor — Recipes

## Overview
The Vaporworks Processor converts fluid inputs (primarily water) into pressurized steam and other gases for use in auxiliary processing systems. Steam is a supplemental reagent resource, not a power substitute, and can be distributed through the fluid network to compatible machines.

## Native recipes
| Input Fluid | Output Fluid | Energy (DE) | Time (s) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Water ×1000 mB | Steam ×1000 mB | 2400 | 4 | Converts water into pressurized steam for auxiliary processing. |
| Cryofluid ×1000 mB | Steam ×1500 mB | 4800 | 6 | Vaporizes cryofluid into high-pressure steam with increased yield. |

## Machine Specifications
- **Input Type:** Fluid
- **Output Type:** Fluid
- **Energy Capacity:** 9.6M DE
- **Base Energy Cost:** 2,400 DE per operation
- **Processing Rate:** 12,000 DE/t base
- **Upgrade Slots:** 2 (Speed, Hyper, Efficiency upgrades supported)

## Features
- Dual fluid tanks (input and output)
- Supports capsule-based fluid input/output
- Compatible with fluid network (pipes and reinforced cables)
- Overclock system compatible for increased throughput
- No item processing — fluid-only operation

## Usage Notes
- Steam is intended as an auxiliary reagent, not a primary energy source
- Output steam can be stored in capsules or distributed via fluid network
- Higher tier inputs (e.g., cryofluid) provide increased steam yields
- Future recipes may introduce additional gas types beyond steam
