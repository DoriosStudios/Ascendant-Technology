# Cloner — Recipes

The Cloner always produces 1 original item + 1 copy (total ×2).

**Fluid:** Liquified Aetherium, consumed at **50 mB per second** of recipe time (time is defined by the recipe).

## Cost per rarity (kDE per second)
- common: 10
- uncommon: 48
- rare: 240
- epic: 1200
- legendary: 6000
- mythic: 30000
