Version 0.6.0 delivers world ore generation, recipe tweaks, and cleaner UIs across the machine lineup. With that, Titanium and Aetherium ores are now naturally integrated into the world.

## BLOCKS
### Machines
- Catalyst Weaver
    - It now has its own texture.
- Energizer
    - Energy Capacity increased from 12.2 MDE to 25.6 MDE.
    - It now has its own texture.

### Ores
- Titanium and Aetherium can now be found in the world. These ores are naturally generated under the following conditions:
  - **Titanium**: 
     - Overworld: Found between Y-32 and Y-64 in the Overworld as Deepslate Titanium Ore.
  - **Aetherium**:
     - Overworld: Found below Y-56 in the Overworld as Deepslate Aetherium Ore; 
     - The End: It spawns naturally at the end, with diverse heights and quantities, as End Aetherium Ore.

## ITEMS
- Crafting catalog reorganized
   - Now, there are groups for machines and capsules (cryo, liquid Aetherium, dark matter).

## RECIPES
- Added more Energizer recipes for Energized Iron Ingot.
- The following blocks have had their recipes modified or added:
  - Absolute Container
  - Cloner
  - Cryo Chamber
  - Energizer
  - Liquifier
  - Residue Processor

## UI/UX
- Updated UIs for Cryo Chamber, Energizer and Network Center.
- Chest screens now route to machine panels; new borderless backgrounds.
- EN/PT text expanded for new blocks and items.

## BUG FIXES
- Fixed Catalyst Weaver consuming items even if there is no output
- Liquifier liquid transfer speed is no longer SLOW
